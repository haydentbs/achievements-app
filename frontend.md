## **Frontend Plan**
### **Tech Stack**
- **Framework:** Deno Fresh (for minimal, performant UI)
- **UI Library:** Tailwind CSS for styling
- **State Management:** Signals (for managing local state efficiently)
- **Routing:** Fresh’s built-in Islands architecture
- **Authentication:** OAuth2 (Google, GitHub) and email/password

### **Key Features**
1. **User Interface**
   - Home page with featured achievements
   - Profile page with achievements, personal goals, and settings
   - Achievement feed with friends' updates
   - Leaderboards and progress insights
   - Responsive design for mobile and desktop

2. **Achievement Logging & Management**
   - Form-based input for logging achievements
   - Goal-setting interface with progress tracking
   - Privacy controls for achievements (public, private, friends-only)

3. **Community & Social Features**
   - Friend requests and group management
   - Likes, comments, and reactions
   - Challenge participation UI

4. **Analytics & Visualizations**
   - Personalized progress charts
   - Global comparison statistics

5. **Notifications & Reminders**
   - UI for push/email notifications
   - Streak tracking and reminders

### **Frontend Development Phases**
1. **Component Design & Prototyping** (Wireframing, UI components)
2. **Page Layouts & Routing** (Home, Profile, Feed, Leaderboards)
3. **State Management Implementation** (User auth, achievements, feed updates)
4. **Interactive Features** (Forms, Modals, Social interactions)
5. **Optimization & Testing** (Performance, accessibility, responsiveness)
